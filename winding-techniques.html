Why winding mattered

Hair length, the diameter of the curl and the distance from the scalp affected the suitability of a procedure. Different winding patterns required compatible clamps, heaters and handling methods.

Croquignole: from ends to scalp

Croquignole winding wraps hair from the ends toward the scalp. The method is not automatically attributable to one inventor: priority, naming and commercial spread need separate evidence.

Josef Mayer and flat winding

Josef Mayer’s US Patent 1,619,794 (filed 19 March 1925, granted 1 March 1927) describes a heating appliance suited to flat-wound hair. Its lateral-opening heated shells address the practical problem of fitting a heater around the curler.

What was new?

The comparison should distinguish a winding method from the apparatus designed to work with it. A patented heater is not, by itself, proof that its inventor originated the entire winding technique.
