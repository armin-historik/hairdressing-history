What do the sources actually prove?

A working catalogue of technical claims, supporting documents and open questions.

← Return to the Innovation Atlas

Patents describe claimed inventions. They do not by themselves demonstrate manufacture, commercial adoption, technical superiority or direct influence. Entries below are research-status notes, not a definitive priority ranking.

How to read this register

Document identified

A dated primary document can be consulted, such as a patent specification. This confirms what the document states, not commercial success.

Object or image lead

A photograph or catalogue may establish appearance or marketing. Its date, provenance and rights must still be checked.

Open attribution

A person, name or system appears in historical research, but a precise invention or date has not yet been established here.

These categories describe the current documentation in this project, not an absolute verdict on historical importance.

Josef Mayer

Patent identified

Technical idea: Lateral-opening heater for flat-wound hair

Evidence and limits: US 1,619,794: filed 19 March 1925; granted 1 March 1927. A patent for an apparatus associated with flat-wound hair, not proof that Mayer originated all flat winding.

Source type to examine: Patent text and drawings

Open question: Production, adoption and priority of the winding method remain unestablished.
