THE INITIATIVE

Research rooted in historical sources.

The International Hairdressing History Project is an independent, archive-based research initiative examining the history of hairdressing and its wider technological, social and industrial contexts.

Its principal focus is approximately 1880–1950, while earlier origins and later developments are considered where relevant.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

Evidence first.
Questions welcome.

Historical claims should be traceable to their sources. Primary documents, contemporary reports and patents are considered alongside secondary literature. Uncertain attributions and unresolved questions are identified.

This is an independent research initiative, not an affiliated university or public museum. Decorative graphics on this homepage are illustrative, not archival objects.
