THE INITIATIVE

Research rooted
in historical sources.

The International Hairdressing History Project is an independent, archive-based research initiative dedicated to the history of hairdressing and its wider professional, technological and industrial contexts.

Its work examines historical documents, patents, trade publications, professional networks and material culture. The initiative distinguishes documented evidence from interpretation and identifies open research questions.

Research focus: approximately 1880–1950, with earlier origins and later developments considered where relevant.

RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

Evidence first.
Questions welcome.

Historical claims should be traceable to their sources. Primary documents, contemporary reports and patents are considered alongside secondary literature. Uncertain attributions and unresolved questions are identified as such.

This is an independent research initiative, not an affiliated university or public museum.
